TYPHOON ODETTE

Learn about the impact of Typhoon Odette (Rai) and the importance of preparing before a powerful storm.

Cebu CityLapu-Lapu CityMandaue City

About this Calamity

Typhoon Odette, internationally known as Typhoon Rai, was one of the strongest typhoons to hit the Philippines in December 2021. It entered the country on December 16, 2021, bringing powerful winds, heavy rainfall, and large waves. Several areas in the Visayas and Mindanao were severely affected.

The typhoon damaged thousands of houses, buildings, schools, roads, and other important structures. Many trees and electric posts were knocked down by strong winds, causing widespread power interruptions. Some communities also lost access to clean water, communication services, and transportation.

Many families were forced to leave their homes and stay in evacuation centers for safety. Farmers and fishermen lost their sources of income because crops, boats, and fishing equipment were damaged. Some people also lost their homes and important belongings.

Typhoon Odette showed how dangerous strong typhoons can be, especially when communities are not fully prepared. It reminds everyone to follow weather updates, prepare emergency supplies, and evacuate when authorities advise them to do so.

Possible Consequences

  • 1,497,214 houses damaged nationwide
  • 362,809 houses totally destroyed nationwide
  • 1,134,405 houses partially damaged nationwide
  • Power, water, communication, roads, and livelihoods were disrupted

The house-damage figures shown are nationwide totals, not Cebu City, Lapu-Lapu City, or Mandaue City figures.

How Should We Prepare?

  • Prepare food, drinking water, medicine, a flashlight, batteries, and important documents.
  • Listen to official weather updates from PAGASA and local authorities.
  • Secure roofs, windows, and loose objects around your home.
  • Know your nearest evacuation center and evacuation route.
  • Evacuate early when authorities advise you to do so.
